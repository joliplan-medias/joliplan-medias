Our method

Joli Plan creates wedding spreadsheets for Excel and Google Sheets, one per country: France, Germany, Spain, Italy and the United Kingdom.

Each article uses the reference points from our spreadsheets and their filled-in example. The figures are starting values: replace them with your own quotes.

For official procedures, we follow the country's official source and cite it. Rules can vary by register office: always confirm with yours.

Our articles are prepared with the help of AI tools, then read and checked before publication. Spotted a mistake? Write to us from our Etsy shop.
