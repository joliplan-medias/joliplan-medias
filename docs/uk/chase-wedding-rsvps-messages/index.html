Chasing wedding RSVPs: two kind messages to copy

Updated on 25/10/2026

The reply-by date has passed and a few guests are still quiet. The simplest fix is a short, warm message with a new date. Here are two templates to copy.

See the wedding planner on Etsy ›

In this article

  1. By text
  2. By email
  3. How to use them well

By text

Hi [Name]! Just a little reminder about our wedding on [date]: could you let us know by [date] if you can make it? Can't wait to see you!

By email

Hi [Name], we're finalising numbers and the seating plan with our caterer. Could you let us know by [date] if you can join us? Please also tell us about any dietary needs or allergies.

How to use them well

  • Replace the details in square brackets and add something personal.
  • Always give a new reply-by date: a clear deadline is more likely to be met.
  • Use the channel each guest uses: text for close friends, email for everyone else.
  • Log every reply as soon as it arrives, so you never ask twice.
