What to take from it

Catering and venue hire together come to £14,922, just over half of the budget. That is where a change of date, menu or guest numbers has the biggest effect.

Keep a contingency line of about 5%. It absorbs small surprises without touching the other lines.

How to adapt these figures to your wedding

  • Replace each amount with your first quote as soon as you have it.
  • If one line goes over, take the difference from another line, not from the contingency.
  • Recalculate whenever guest numbers change: catering follows the number of covers.
  • Keep every line on one page so you can see at a glance what is already allocated.
