Wedding day emergency kit: the checklist to tick off

Updated on 25/10/2026

Something small almost always goes wrong on a wedding day: a button, a heel, a flat battery. An emergency kit packed in good time helps with most of these little problems.

The essentials

  • Wedding rings
  • Registrar's paperwork
  • Printed speeches
  • Supplier payment envelopes

Little lifesavers

  • Needle, thread, pins
  • Plasters, painkillers
  • Tissues
  • Flat shoes to dance in

Just in case

  • Phone charger, power bank
  • Umbrellas
  • Water and snacks
  • Spare tights

Who carries the kit

Hand the kit to someone who stays close to you all day, such as a witness. That way you don't have to think about any of it.

Write a name on each payment envelope so none gets mixed up. This list covers the essentials: add anything you personally need, such as medication.
